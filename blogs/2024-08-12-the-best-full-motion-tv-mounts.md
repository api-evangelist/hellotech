Nothing will make your new big-screen TV look better than mounting it on the wall. With an articulating wall mount (also known as a full-motion mount), you can swivel, tilt, extend, retract, and even rotate your TV. This way, you’ll always have the best seat in the house, no matter where you’re sitting.
